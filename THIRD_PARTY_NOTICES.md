# Third-Party Notices

Textify 4.47.1 has **no third-party runtime JavaScript dependencies** and bundles no external solver package, framework, web font, third-party source package, or third-party media asset.

The Stipple and Single-Line/TSP implementations use general mathematical and research concepts such as weighted/centroidal Voronoi stippling, blue-noise/Poisson placement, ordered dithering, image-gradient analysis, heuristic TSP routing, local 2-opt improvement, and TSP-art density methods.

Research/reference attribution is recorded in `ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md` and documented in detail in `REFERENCES_AND_INFLUENCES.md`. Those references are not bundled source code.
